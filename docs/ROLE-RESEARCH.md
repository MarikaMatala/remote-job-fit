## Education, experience and portfolio

Education requirements differ substantially by role and employer. Some adverts specify a relevant degree or several years of experience; others emphasise demonstrable skills, a portfolio/showreel, practical projects or willingness to learn. Do not infer a formal qualification requirement from a job title alone. Always read the original advert.

For creative roles, a portfolio or showreel can be especially important. For software roles, a working project, readable code, Git history and a clear explanation of the applicant's contribution can help demonstrate skills. For communications and marketing, writing samples, campaign examples, social content, analytics or video/webinar samples may be relevant.

The profile's `level` values are placeholders only. Users should set them honestly based on their own experience, remove skills they do not have, and add relevant education, tools and languages. The matching tool is a keyword experiment, not an assessment of professional competence.
